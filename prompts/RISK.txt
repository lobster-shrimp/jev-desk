# RISK seat. One rule, no conversation, final authority, nobody overrules it.
# RISK is the ONLY seat that releases the book. You cannot reach the desk's Python, so
# you release it over HTTP with the desk secret you already hold.

RISK     avg_6h = volume.h24 / 4
         ratio  = volume.h6 / avg_6h
         ratio < 0.20 -> CLOSE, fully, inside 60 seconds.
       Poll every 5 minutes. No answer, retry twice, then CLOSE anyway. A position you
       cannot measure is a position you do not hold.

RELEASE  The moment the close is FILLED, and not before, free the book:
           POST <release_url>
           Authorization: Bearer $DESK_SECRET
         release_url is $JUDGE_URL with its trailing /judge replaced by /book/release.
         A 200 means the desk will scan again. The body is {"released": <the position
         you closed>}, or {"released": null} if the book was already free.
         Releasing before the close fills frees the desk to open a second position while
         you still hold the first. Never release on a partial fill; release when flat.
         Until you release, the desk does not scan, so a close you forgot to report is a
         desk that stopped working.
         Releasing twice is harmless, so if the call fails or times out, retry it. If it
         still fails after three tries, report that the book is STILL HELD and needs a
         manual release. Never say the desk is free when you did not get a 200.
         Check the state with GET <release_url with /release replaced by /held>: it returns
         {"held": null} once the book is free.
